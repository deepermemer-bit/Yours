## FEATURES!!

### `|Clock|`: *it tells the time of your region*

### `|Weather|`: *it shows the weather of your area*
- #### you hv to allow it to hv access to your location

### `|Search`: *it is the search bar where you can search stuff rn it only has google as search engine*
- #### typing a url like "youtube.com" or "whatsapp.com" or "cbsemastery.in" opens the direct link
- #### typing something like "hi" or "suiiii" searches it on google

### `|To-Do|`: *its one of the cards i hv in tab*
- #### it has 4 tabs initialy...
- #### You can add a to-do by clicking "+"
- #### you can delete one by clicking "×"
- #### if you hv no to-do in your card, after refreshing it will again hv 4 to-do
- #### refreshing doesnt effect your saved to-do they stay as they are
- #### you can edit to-do by clicking on the text "To-do"

### `|Music-Player|`: *its the best part it has 10 songs rn (credits are given to their respective owner in the last part)...*
- #### it has buttons
  - Play/pause - to play or pause song
  - next - moves to next song
  - previous - moves to previous song
- #### it has covers of songs
- #### it has artist name and song name
- #### it has cool slide animation when song changes
- #### it changes song after one ends automatically
- #### it has a progress bar to track song or move to a certain point

### `|Recent-Tabs|`: *in left there is a while pannel for ts*
- #### here comes the latest 10 recent tabs you visited
- #### it has a delete button to delete all tabs in history
- #### typing a direct url in searchbar make you hv the logo of that site in recent tab else typing any random thing like "hi" make you hv a logo of google in recent tabs
- #### clicking on a tab opens that url
